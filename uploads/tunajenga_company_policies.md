# Tunajenga Company Limited - Company Policies

## HR Policies
Tunajenga Company Limited is committed to maintaining a fair, inclusive, collaborative, and performance-driven workplace.
All employment and team decisions are made fairly and without discrimination.
* Equal opportunity workplace
* Bi-annual performance and growth reviews
* Clear career and skill progression framework
* Strict anti-harassment and respectful-workplace policy

## Leave Policy
Team members are encouraged to maintain a healthy work-life balance.
* Annual Leave: 18 working days per year
* Sick Leave: 10 working days per year
* Casual Leave: 6 working days per year
* Maternity Leave: As per applicable statutory norms
* Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to 30 days

## Workplace Conduct
Everyone at Tunajenga Company Limited is expected to act professionally, ethically, and responsibly at all times.
* Maintain confidentiality of client, company, partner, and project information
* Avoid conflicts of interest
* No acceptance of bribes or unethical gifts
* Follow site safety, operational blueprints, and data-protection policies

## Disciplinary Actions
Any serious or repeated violation of company policies may result in disciplinary action.
* Written warning
* Suspension
* Termination of employment in severe cases
